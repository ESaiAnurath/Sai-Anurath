# Student Portfolio Template (SR University)
Your own portfolio website, free, live in about 30 minutes, built entirely in the browser. No software to install.
Your link will look like **https://your-github-id.github.io**. Put it on your resume and your LinkedIn.

## Part 1: the website (about 20 min)
1. Log in to **github.com** (create a free account if you don't have one; choose a professional username).
2. On this page, click the green **Use this template** → **Create a new repository**.
3. Repository name: **exactly** `your-github-id.github.io` (for example `ravi-kumar.github.io`). Keep it **Public** → **Create repository**.
4. Click **index.html** → the ✏️ pencil icon. Change the 7 parts marked `EDIT` (name, headline, about, skills, projects, certifications, achievements).
   - Only list projects, skills and certificates that are really yours. Interviewers ask about every line.
   - To add a project, copy a whole `<article> … </article>` block.
5. Click **Commit changes** (green button) → **Commit changes**.
6. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Branch" choose **main** and **/(root)** → **Save**. Wait 1–2 minutes.
7. Open `https://your-github-id.github.io` on your phone. If it shows your page, you're done. 🎉
   (Optional: upload your resume as `resume.pdf` with **Add file → Upload files**, so the Resume button works.)

## Part 2: LinkedIn (needed for approval)
- ✅ A clear **profile photo** (face, plain background)
- ✅ A **headline**, e.g. *"B.Tech CSE @ SR University | Cloud & AWS | Python"*
- ✅ **At least 100 connections**: connect with classmates, seniors, faculty and club members
- ✅ Add your portfolio link under **Contact info → Website**

## Part 3: the LinkedIn post (from YOUR account)
Copy, edit, and post. It must have **2 tags** (type `@` and pick from the list so they turn blue) and **2 hashtags**.

> I built my portfolio website to showcase my projects, skills and certifications 🚀
> 🔗 https://your-github-id.github.io
> Highlights: [your best project in one line] · [a certification or skill]
> Thanks to @SR University and my faculty mentor @[mentor's name] for the guidance.
> #StudentPortfolio #CloudComputing

## Part 4: send to your faculty mentor
Your **name, roll number, portfolio link and LinkedIn post link**. Your mentor checks everything and submits it for approval.
